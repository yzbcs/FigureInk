# Figure grammar

## Semantic roles

| Role | Visual treatment | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Focal mechanism | strongest border and saturated fill | paper's novel intervention |
| Baseline or prior practice | neutral fill and thinner border | comparison state |
| Data or input | pale cool fill | observed input or source |
| Intermediate state | medium cool fill | transformed representation |
| Output or evidence | pale green fill | result or decision |
| Failure or warning | warm pale fill | limitation, error, or conflict |

Use role semantics consistently across all panels.

## Relations

- solid arrow: data or execution flow;
- dashed arrow: supervision, optional dependency, or planned link;
- curved return arrow: feedback or iteration;
- bracket or paired panels: contrast;
- boundary box: subsystem, stage, or shared context.

Label every relation whose meaning is not obvious from direction alone.

## Critique order

1. **Semantic:** Are entities and relations true?
2. **Narrative:** Is the intended reading order unambiguous?
3. **Visual:** Are hierarchy, spacing, typography, and color balanced?
4. **Production:** Does the figure remain legible at final size and in grayscale?

Never approve a visual pass when the semantic pass fails.
