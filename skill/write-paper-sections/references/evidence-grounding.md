# Evidence grounding

## Claim status

- `hypothesis`: proposed explanation not yet tested;
- `planned`: experiment specified but not executed;
- `partial`: some required cells or conditions are missing;
- `measured`: result exists with complete denominator and provenance;
- `contradicted`: observed evidence conflicts with the claim.

## Evidence strength

- `direct`: the artifact measures the claimed quantity under the claimed scope;
- `proxy`: related measurement with a stated inferential gap;
- `qualitative`: cases or observations without aggregate support;
- `external`: cited prior evidence;
- `none`: unsupported.

Only `measured` claims with direct evidence should be phrased as headline empirical findings.
